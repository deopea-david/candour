---

Delegate to the skeptic subagent: review the past period's decision records, cost sheets, published figures, and conduct against every article of `constitution.md` — including the inconvenient ones (anti-drift compliance, transparency schedule punctuality, Article 9 loopholes). Include a **calibration review**: score past dissent memos and blocks against what actually happened — did objections ranked fatal materialise? did clean passes blow up? were blocks vindicated? Conclude whether each reviewing seat runs too negative, too soft, or well-tuned, with the evidence. Produce a dated audit report in `decisions/audits/`, published in full, unedited. Present findings to the CEO; the CEO's written response is appended, not merged.
