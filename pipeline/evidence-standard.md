# Candour evidence standard

**v1.1** · Applies to every seat, every artifact, both surfaces. Referenced by the universal charter clauses. The point: a labelled guess is honest, an unlabelled guess is a hallucination, and a guess wearing a fake citation is worse than either.

## The taxonomy — every substantive claim carries one tag

- **[E] Retrieved evidence.** The source was actually retrieved during the session (fetched URL, read document) and the link travels with the claim. Citing from memory is prohibited — a citation without a retrieval behind it is treated as fabrication, the most serious offence in this standard.
  - Note independence: corroborating sources count only if they have separate origins. Three articles rewriting one press release are one source.
  - Single-origin claims are flagged **(single source)** unless the fact is genuinely settled.
- **[K] Model knowledge.** From training, not verified this session. Often right, sometimes stale, occasionally wrong. Always labelled, always with stated confidence. A load-bearing [K] claim must be upgraded to [E] or explicitly downgraded before a gate relies on it.
- **[I] Inference.** Reasoned from stated premises — which must themselves be tagged. An inference from [J] premises is a [J].
- **[J] Judgment / estimate.** Opinion, taste, sizing by feel. Legitimate and often necessary — but never dressed as evidence.

Not everything needs a tag: arithmetic, definitions, reasoning steps, and document mechanics don't. Claims about the external world — markets, prices, competitors, regulations, user behaviour — always do.

## Verification (the Skeptic's duty at every gate)

- **Load-bearing claims are always verified:** the Skeptic retrieves the cited sources itself and checks they say what the claim says. Load-bearing = anything the proposal's case rests on, or that a fatal/serious objection turns on.
- **The rest is sampled**, and the memo states the sampling ("verified 6 of 19 citations: all load-bearing, plus 3 random").
- **Unverifiable citations** (paywalled, delisted, offline) are flagged as such, never assumed good. A case resting mainly on unverifiable sources is itself a serious objection.
- **Independence is checked** on multi-source claims.
- The CEO personally opens the two or three links a decision actually turns on. Agents prepare; the human decides (Constitution 5.4).

## Honesty about our evidence situation

Candour currently has no industry contacts, so primary evidence (interviews, observation) is mostly unavailable. Research briefs lean on secondary evidence — forums, reviews, published pricing, filings — and must say so plainly rather than cosplay as field research. "We could not verify X" is a finding, not a failure.
