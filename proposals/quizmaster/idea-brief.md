# Idea brief — Quizmaster

**Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Origin:** CEO spark via `/scout` (round 2 — promoted over a Skeptic kill) · **Status:** spark → awaiting research

## The idea in two sentences

A printable pub-quiz pack — question sheet, answer sheet, tie-breaker, scoresheet — in which **every answer carries a retrieved source link**, so the host can defend any answer challenged from the floor. The product is not the questions; it is that the answers are verified, which is Candour's evidence standard turned into a consumer feature.

## Who it's for, and what they do today instead

UK pub quiz hosts in the first instance — order of 10,000–20,000, from a figure of ~22,445 weekly pub quizzes and 42% of pubs hosting one [E, search-snippet only, single source, and the hard number is from a 2008 survey — indicative only] (https://www.orangejelly.co.uk/licensees-guide/quiz-night-101). Secondarily teachers, care home activity coordinators, office social organisers and families, who pay less and buy less often.

Today they: write their own; download free ready-to-run packs from at least six UK sites (freepubquiz.co.uk, woodenspoonquizzes.co.uk, justprintables.co.uk, quizondemand.co.uk/free-stuff, quizdemon.co.uk, quizsupplies.co.uk) [E, search-snippet only]; or buy at £2.00–£4.50 — Instant Quizzes £4.50/pack with subscriptions from £21 [E] (https://www.instant-quizzes.co.uk/collections/quiz-night), Quiz On Demand from £2.00 [E] (https://quizondemand.co.uk/pub-quiz-subscription-packages/).

## Why this fits Candour (which principles it serves)

- **1.3 / Article 4 — honest by default.** "Every answer cited" is a substantiable claim in a category where nobody makes one. It is the evidence standard as product.
- **1.1 — genuinely helpful** *if* the pain is real: a host who cannot defend an answer loses the room.
- No cold start: packs sell individually from day one. No lock-in, no subscription required, nothing to cancel.

## What would make this NOT worth doing

Written honestly at spark stage. **Both of the reasons this was killed in the scout remain unanswered, and promotion did not answer them.** The Skeptic's objections stand in full:

1. **The differentiator is unevidenced.** The Research Analyst went looking for evidence that quiz hosts are troubled by wrong answers — Reddit, trade coverage, reviews, forums — and **found none**. The nearest material was generic (a Jeopardy clue error, an AI quiz app's own hallucination disclaimer, the academic hallucination literature). None of it shows a buyer will pay for citations. This is the load-bearing assumption of the whole idea.
2. **The verification cost plausibly exceeds the market price.** A 60-question pack needs 60 retrievals plus a check that each source says what the answer claims, plus a human spot-check to make "verified" honest under Article 4 — estimated [J] £1.50–£6 per pack against a market clearing at £2.00–£4.50. Under the 20% target and 30% hard cap, that is at or beyond break-even before hosting or labour. **If a verified pack costs more than about £2.50 to produce, the margin cap kills this regardless of what any research finds.**
3. Free-and-good is the real competitor, not free-and-bad.
4. Picture rounds would import image rights — out of scope by default.

**CVO position, recorded once per charter:** objection 2 is arithmetic, not research, and it should be settled before the research brief consumes three weeks. See the commission below.
