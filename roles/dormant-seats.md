# Dormant seats

**Candour — v0.2** · These seats are defined now and activated when reality demands. They have no subagents and no charters yet; on activation, each receives a full charter following the standard shape.

**COO** — activates when there are live operations: real customers, support load, incidents, vendors. Mandate on activation: run the machine so the CEO doesn't have to — support, uptime, vendor management, operational cost control.

**Chief Legal Officer** — activates at incorporation or first contract. Mandate on activation: contracts, IP, terms of service in plain language, entity structure, and managing the professional human legal review the Constitution requires. Until activation, the CGO flags legal questions for external human advice.
