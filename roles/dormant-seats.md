# Dormant seats

**Candour — v0.3** · These seats are defined now and activated when reality demands. They have no subagents and no charters yet; on activation, each receives a full charter following the standard shape.

**COO** — activates when there are live operations: real customers, support load, incidents, vendors. Mandate on activation: run the machine so the CEO doesn't have to — support, uptime, vendor management, operational cost control. **Boundary with the Coordinator:** the COO runs *operations* (customers, incidents, vendors); it does not coordinate the agent seats. That is the Coordinator's job (`roles/coordinator.md`: *"You coordinate the agent seats only; operations … belong to the dormant COO"*), and it runs only in a session where the CEO has typed `/coordinator` (D14). Neither seat takes the other's work. On activation, the COO's charter states how it works with the Coordinator, and any overlap between them goes to the CEO.

**Chief Legal Officer** — activates at incorporation or first contract. Mandate on activation: contracts, IP, terms of service in plain language, entity structure, and managing the professional human legal review the Constitution requires. Until activation, the CGO flags legal questions for external human advice.
